# Countdown visual references

The result is an original mechanical reveal, not a reproduction of a verified VH1 countdown bumper. All five numbers occupy one track; shutters expose the selected bay. No network logos or downloaded reference footage are included.

## Dated broadcast references

- [YO CLAS! MTV DAYS collection](https://www.yoclas.com/projects/mtv/mtvoldiesbutgoldies/) explicitly labels two frames MTV DAY 2006. Inspected frames show a city-scale mechanical figure and riveted, weathered metal with deep beveled typography. The portfolio's current image upload date is not the work's date. Neighboring 2005 and 2008 projects were excluded.
- [UVPHACTORY VH1 Metal Month teaser](https://vimeo.com/98149873) identifies the 2006 package. Its inspected frame supplies extruded metallic lettering and dramatic lighting. [Stash issue 21, May 2006](https://www.stashmedia.tv/pdfs/Stash21_booklet_screen.pdf), entry 21.12, independently dates and credits that work. These are frame observations, not a claim to have reconstructed the original motion.
- [Contemporary MTV VMA staging report](https://www.livedesignonline.com/staging-rental/kish-rigging-sets-mtv-vmas-motion-cyberhoist), October 2006, describes moving framed video panels. This is physical staging context, not evidence of the countdown package itself.

The exact 2006 VH1 VSPOT countdown animation was not verified. Later MTV global rebrands and undated countdown reels were not treated as 2006 evidence.

## Sister-site adaptation

Inspected [the production's landing page](https://www.artisticaccessibility.com/2006) and [countdown page](https://www.artisticaccessibility.com/2006/countdown). The landing page combines magazine collage and silver iPod hardware. The countdown uses cobalt window chrome, cream surfaces, black broadcast graphics, acid green and pink accents.

The revised machine keeps the mechanical structure but replaces military olive, rust and amber with silver-blue casing, green and pink lighting, white-edged stars and the existing local /2006/bg.jpg collage. No external image requests are needed during playback. The numeral keeps dimensional chrome and a thick cream edge to remain legible over the collage. The local preview offers all five numbers and pause/replay; it does not change show state.

## Motion

For the default three seconds, the carriage travels behind shutters during approximately the first 1.3 seconds; the aperture opens from 1.02 to 1.71 seconds. Camera roll and zoom settle into the selected numeral, which holds until the shared deadline. The video then appears through the existing 220 ms exit. These proportions scale with the configured intro duration. Pause, reconnect and reduced motion retain their existing shared-clock behavior.
