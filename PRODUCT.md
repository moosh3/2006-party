# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Online viewers watching the live 2006 performance, cast and production operators running the synchronized broadcast. Viewers move between watching, chatting, voting and short trivia interactions on laptops and phones.

## Product Purpose

Keep the shared program and TRL video vote central. Trivia and cast superlatives add lightweight participation between video votes, without requiring audience accounts or adding operator busywork.

## Brand Personality

Playful, nostalgic, familiar. Preserve the existing AIM, Windows XP and 2006 Mac desktop references, including the fictional desktop apps.

## Anti-references

Do not replace the period desktop with a generic modern dashboard or let decorative windows obscure participation. Do not turn the minigame into the primary show.

## Design Principles

- The broadcast and main TRL vote come first.
- Make the next audience action easy to find.
- Keep administration predictable and protect live operations.
- Preserve the period character while improving readability and feedback.
- Fit the same experience to phones without requiring desktop precision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize keyboard access, visible focus, readable controls, mobile usability and reduced-motion support, as confirmed by the user. Preserve captions and existing accessibility paths. Browser checks do not constitute a full accessibility certification.
